Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

SpendWise (“we”, “us”) is a personal expense tracking app. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It is written to align with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is stored in hashed form, not as plain text.
  • Information you add: transactions (type, amount, description, category, date and optional note), categories, budgets and your currency setting.
  • Technical data: your browser’s local storage is used to keep you signed in and to remember your light or dark theme. Our hosting and authentication providers may also keep standard technical logs such as IP address, device type and timestamps for security and reliability.

We do not ask for your bank account numbers, card numbers or bank login details, and SpendWise does not connect to your bank.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To store your entries and show your balances, budgets and reports.
  • To send account emails, such as email confirmation.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and prevent misuse.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for, and, where required, with your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, although this may mean we can no longer provide the service to you.

4. Who we share data with

We use trusted service providers to run SpendWise. They process data only to provide their services to us:

  • Supabase, for the database and authentication.
  • Vercel, for hosting the website and app.
  • An email delivery provider, for sending account emails.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights and the safety of users.

5. Where your data is stored

Your account data is stored with our database provider in the United Kingdom (London region), which is outside Nigeria. By using SpendWise you understand that your data is transferred to and stored there, and we rely on our providers’ safeguards for this.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete all your transactions and budgets at any time using “Clear all data” in Settings. To delete your account entirely, contact us and we will remove your data within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections, email-confirmed accounts and database rules that allow each account to access only its own records. No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have rights over your personal data, including the right to be informed, to access it, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. You can export your transactions and download a full backup from Settings at any time. To exercise any other right, contact us. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

9. Children

SpendWise is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make important changes, we will tell you through the website or by email.
